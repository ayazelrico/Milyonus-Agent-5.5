# Lead Generation

Quality of the list beats volume of the send. Define who, then find them.

## 1. Define the ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
- Firmographics: industry, company size, geography, tech stack, funding stage.
- Persona: role/title, seniority, the problem they own, their buying trigger.
- Write it down; every lead is scored against it.

## 2. Find them (sources)
- Public professional networks (search by title + company filters).
- Company sites, team/about pages, job postings (hiring = a buying signal).
- Communities, event attendee lists, podcast guests, GitHub/portfolios (technical).
- Data providers/enrichment APIs for firmographics + verified emails.

## 3. Qualify (before you spend a touch)
- Fit: matches the ICP? Score high/med/low.
- Timing/trigger: recent hire, funding, launch, tech change, expansion.
- Reachability: a valid, verified business email; not a role-based catch-all.

## 4. Enrich & verify
- Verify emails (avoid bounces — they wreck deliverability; see that skill).
- Fill merge variables you'll actually use in personalization.

## Ethics & compliance
- Collect only business-relevant data from legitimate sources; respect robots.txt
  and terms. Under GDPR/CCPA have a lawful basis and honor deletion requests.
- Don't scrape or aggregate personal data indiscriminately.
