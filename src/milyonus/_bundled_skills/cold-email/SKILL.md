# Cold Email

Goal: earn a reply, not make a sale. Short, relevant, one ask.

## Structure (keep under ~90 words)
1. **Subject:** 2–4 words, lowercase, specific, no clickbait. Test 2 variants.
   Good: "quick question about {topic}", "{their company} + {outcome}".
2. **Opener (1 line):** a real, specific observation about *them* — not "I hope
   you're well". Reference a trigger (a hire, a launch, a post, a job posting).
3. **Value (1–2 lines):** the outcome you drive for people like them, with a
   concrete proof point or number. No feature dumps.
4. **CTA (1 line):** one low-friction ask. "Worth a quick look?" beats "book a
   30-min call". Interest-based CTA > time-based CTA on first touch.

## Personalization tiers
- **Tier 1 (best):** a sentence only true for this person (their post, their product).
- **Tier 2:** true for their role + company segment (merge variables).
- **Tier 3:** true for their industry. Never send Tier-3-only cold email at scale.

## Rules
- One idea per email. Plain text, no images/tracking pixels in the first touch
  (hurts deliverability and trust).
- Write like a human to one person; read it aloud.
- Compliance: identify yourself, include a real opt-out, honor it. See
  `email-deliverability` and `outreach-sequencing`.
- Never fabricate a shared connection, a stat, or a "per our last conversation".
