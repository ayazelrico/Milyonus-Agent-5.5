# Outreach Sequencing

Most replies come from follow-ups — but a sequence is a conversation, not a
countdown. Add value each touch; stop when it's clearly no.

## A sane multi-touch cadence (email-led, ~2–3 weeks)
1. **Day 0** — first touch (see `cold-email`): specific, one ask.
2. **Day 3** — short bump: new angle or a proof point, not "just following up".
3. **Day 7** — value add: a resource/insight relevant to their problem, soft ask.
4. **Day 12** — different medium (LinkedIn touch / short note).
5. **Day 18** — polite break-up: "closing the loop — should I stop?" (often replies).

## Principles
- **Each touch adds something new** — never resend the same message.
- Vary the angle: pain, proof, resource, question, break-up.
- Multi-channel beats single-channel, but don't hit every channel at once.
- **Reply detection stops the sequence** immediately (never keep sending after a reply).
- Cap total touches (≈4–6). Persistence ≠ harassment.

## Compliance
- Honor unsubscribe/opt-out on every step, instantly.
- Identify yourself and your company; no deceptive subject/sender.
- Keep suppression lists (unsubscribed, bounced, replied) and respect them.
