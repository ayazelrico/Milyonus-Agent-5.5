# LinkedIn Outreach

LinkedIn is relationship-first; sell later. Match the channel's norms.

## Connection request
- **Send a note** (personalized) — blank requests get ignored or flagged.
- Keep it under ~200 characters: one specific reason you're connecting. No pitch.
- Reference something real: their post, mutual group, shared interest, a talk.

## After they accept (don't pitch immediately)
1. Thank + a genuine, specific comment. Start a conversation.
2. Add value: share a relevant resource, an insight, an intro — no ask.
3. Only when there's a signal of interest, make a soft, relevant offer.

## InMail (paid) tips
- Subject matters; lead with *their* problem, not your product.
- Shorter than email. One question. Interest-based CTA.

## Cadence & etiquette
- Space touches by days, not hours. 2–3 follow-ups max, then stop.
- Comment on their content between messages (warm, not creepy).
- Respect connection/message limits; automation that violates ToS risks bans.
- Personalize; do not blast identical DMs — it reads as spam and gets reported.
