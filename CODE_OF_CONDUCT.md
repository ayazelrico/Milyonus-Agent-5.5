# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) v2.1.
Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the work.
Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers listed in `pyproject.toml`.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, and contributions that violate
this code, and may ban temporarily or permanently for other behaviors deemed
inappropriate.
